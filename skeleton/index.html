The bush

A leafless potted highbush blueberry, filmed on 6 November. 187 frames from one walk around it, rebuilt as 47,307 Gaussian splats.

The skeleton

Extracted from the same capture: 5,242 points, 1,472 tips. Each splat rides on its nearest point.

The motion

Ten vibration modes of a rod model built on this skeleton, tuned on other pulls of the same bush, never on the one shown.
